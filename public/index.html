HOW BASESTOCK10 WORKS

One wallet. One Glider account. Ten-stock exposure.

BaseStock10 is a non-custodial interface for a Glider-managed Base portfolio. You approve every wallet transaction; the app never holds your wallet keys.

01 · CONNECT

Connect a Base wallet

Use a Base-compatible wallet. The app checks whether this wallet already has a portfolio for the selected Glider strategy, so repeat visits keep using the same account.

02 · FUND

Send USDC to your Glider account

On the first investment, Glider creates a personal Base smart account. USDC is transferred directly from your wallet to that Glider-provided address; every later deposit uses the same address.

03 · INDEX & EXECUTE

Glider manages the strategy

After the transfer is indexed, you can ask Glider to rebalance. Glider handles the strategy’s execution; BaseStock10 does not create KyberSwap routes or take custody of your USDC.

04 · VIEW & WITHDRAW

Withdraw indexed assets

Your live positions are read from Glider. Choose an indexed asset, use MAX for the full available balance, then sign Glider’s withdrawal authorization to return assets to your Base wallet.
